## Known issue and stopping note

Country and city-network sets use Wikidata P463 snapshots. Spotchecks prove the saved query rows, but those rows were not cross-checked against every organization’s official roster. Review found discrepancies: EU P463 includes GB and omits NL; ASEAN includes AU; NATO omits AL and NL; OECD has 35 entries. Recent additions (Timor-Leste, Sweden, Romania/Bulgaria and BRICS entrants) are not consistently disputed.
