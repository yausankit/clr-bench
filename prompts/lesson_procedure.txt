When a level rule applies to a labelled class and the report gives only one example value, decide which class the example and the feedback point to and write it into your notes together with the label. Then apply the rule to every value the report describes that belongs to that class, including values that never appeared in feedback, and leave out values that are not in it. Before coding, go through every described value, decide membership for each one, and hard-code the complete member set.
